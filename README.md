# Alzumarud - Emerald

> **Development Status:** 🚧 Active Beta Development

**Alzumarud - Emerald** is a web-based 2D adventure game built around exploration, atmosphere, movement, and peaceful discovery. Instead of focusing on competition, strict objectives, or reaching a definitive finish line, the game encourages players to explore at their own pace, discover new materials, experiment with crafting, and enjoy a relaxing gameplay experience.

Whether you're exploring unfamiliar areas, collecting resources, crafting new items, or simply wandering through the world, **Alzumarud is about enjoying the journey rather than rushing toward the destination.**

> **Transparency Notice:** Approximately 50% of the game's development has involved AI assistance.  
> **AI Tools Credited:** ChatGPT, Claude Sonnet 4.5, GLM Latest, Gemini Flash 3.8, Argon High, and Qwen Latest.

---

## Key Features

- **Exploration Without Pressure** — Discover the world at your own pace, without time limits or strict victory conditions. Prefer a challenge? You can also try to beat your high score.
- **2D Platforming & Movement** — Explore a platforming world focused on movement, traversal, and interaction with your surroundings.
- **Character Customization** — Personalize your character before beginning your adventure.
- **Inventory & Resource Collection** — Collect materials and manage your inventory as you explore.
- **Crafting System** — Discover resources and craft new items to support your journey.
- **Recipe Book** — A recipe-book feature is in development to make discovering and managing crafting recipes easier.
- **Continuous Development** — New features, materials, improvements, and bug fixes are being introduced throughout development.

*Some features and systems may change as development progresses. Additional details about certain features will be revealed in future releases.*

## Windows Desktop Application — Electron

Alzumarud is primarily built using web technologies, including JavaScript. To bring the game to Windows desktops, the project uses **Electron**, allowing the web-based game to run as a desktop application.

The planned Windows distribution will support a portable version and/or an installer-based package, making it easier to install and play without opening a browser.

Desktop packaging and compatibility will continue to improve as the game approaches its first official release.

---

## Android Application — Capacitor

**The Android version is currently under development.**

The project uses **Capacitor** to bring the web-based game to Android devices. The goal is to make Alzumarud available on mobile while adapting the experience to the needs of touchscreen gameplay.

The Android application is planned for release alongside the game's first official release, God willing.

**Please note:**
- There will be no official *Latest Release* in 2026.
- The Android application is still under development and may undergo significant changes.
- **An official iOS version is not planned.**

## Linux Desktop Application — Electron

A Linux desktop version is also planned for the game's first official release. The intended distribution formats include **`.deb` and `.rpm` packages**, subject to compatibility and testing.

I want to be transparent about a limitation: I do not currently own a Linux desktop system. As a result, my ability to test the Linux application directly is limited, and development or troubleshooting may take longer than expected.

Community feedback and reports from Linux users would be valuable in helping improve compatibility and reliability.

## 🚧 Release Roadmap — Version 1.0.0

Alzumarud - Emerald is currently in active beta development. Rather than rushing toward an early official release, development will continue with a focus on expanding the game's features, improving compatibility, optimizing performance, and introducing new ideas and innovations.

### Planned Release: Q3 2027

**The first major official release, `v1.0.0`, is currently planned for Q3 2027 (July–September 2027).**

This milestone is intended to deliver a significantly more complete and refined experience, featuring:

- **Expanded Gameplay:** More features, materials, crafting possibilities, and gameplay systems.
- **Performance Optimization:** Improvements to resource usage, responsiveness, stability, and overall smoothness.
- **Platform Compatibility:** Further development and testing for supported desktop and mobile platforms.
- **Technical Improvements:** Refinements to existing systems, fewer known bugs, and a more reliable gameplay experience.
- **New Innovations:** Additional ideas, features, and improvements intended to make Alzumarud a more distinctive adventure game.

These goals are part of the development direction and are subject to change as the project evolves.

### Why Not Release Earlier?

Alzumarud is an independent project, and its development takes time. The intention is to make version 1.0.0 a meaningful milestone, not simply another version number.

**There will be no official `Latest Release` in 2026.** Development will continue throughout this period, and experimental builds, beta versions, or other development updates may still be published when appropriate.

The Q3 2027 date is a target, not a guaranteed deadline. It may change if additional development, optimization, testing, or compatibility work is needed.

### Desktop & Mobile Availability

The Windows desktop application, Android application, and planned Linux desktop application will continue to be developed toward their intended release milestones. Their final availability and platform support will depend on development progress and testing.

An official iOS version is not planned at this time.

*Thank you for following Alzumarud - Emerald during its development. The goal is to build something worth the wait.*
